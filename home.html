Bayerische Motoren Werke AG (BMW) is an automotive company. It develops, manufactures, and sells automobiles and motorcycles, spare parts and accessories, and engines. The company also offers deposit business, retail customer and dealer financing, insurance, fleet business, and car leasing services. It markets products under the BMW, MINI, Rolls-Royce, Alphabet, John Cooper Works, and Motorrad brands. BMW is involved in the development, manufacturing, assembly, and sale of automobiles and off-road vehicles. The company serves customers in Europe, the Americas, Asia, and other regions. It has production and assembly facilities, and a sales network across the world. BMW is headquartered in Munich, Bayern, Germany.
